#### Infinite Money - Nugget Glitch (Optional)
1. Go to the Nugget Bridge on Route 24, north of Cerulean City.
2. Defeat 5 trainers on the Bridge
3. Defeat the Rocket Grunt at the top to receive a free Nugget.
4. Intentionally lose your battle against the very next trainer (e.g., have a low-level Pokémon in your party so you quickly black out).
5. You will respawn at the Pokémon Center, but the Nugget remains in your bag, and the Rocket Grunt will still be waiting for you.
6. Repeat this process as many times as you want, then sell the Nuggets for P5,000 each to buy TMs or Game Corner coins.  
    Note: Once you officially defeat the Grunt and the next trainer, this glitch ends for this story run

#### Main Story
